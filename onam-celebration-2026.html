🌸 Onam Celebration 2026: Colors of Unity, Harvest & Festive Joy

August 2026 Cogniza Campus, Bangalore 4 min read By Cogniza Culture Team
Share this story:

The spirit of prosperity, harmony, and togetherness came alive at Cogniza as our mentors, engineers, student coordinators, and staff gathered in traditional finery to celebrate the auspicious festival of Onam 2026.

A Tapestry of Tradition & Team Spirit

Onam marks the harvest season and commemorates the eternal homecoming of the legendary King Mahabali. At Cogniza, the celebration was a joyful reflection of our multicultural workforce and inclusive community, bringing everyone together to create timeless memories.

Onam 2026 Highlights

  • Grand Pookkalam Competition: Multi-team floral rangoli created with fresh marigolds, roses, and jasmine
  • Traditional Attire: Team members donned elegant Kerala Kasavu sarees and traditional Mundus
  • Festive Feast & Sweets: Delicious Payasam and festive snacks shared across departments
  • Cultural Performances: Traditional Onam songs, folk games, and interactive group activities
  • Campus Photo Moments: Group photographs capturing the festive colors and bonding

Embracing Cultural Diversity at the Workplace

Festivals at Cogniza are an integral part of our life and culture. They remind us that beyond lines of code, curriculum design, and tech mentorship, our core strength lies in unity, mutual respect, and celebrated camaraderie.

"Celebrating Onam with the entire Cogniza family brought an incredible sense of joy and warmth. The floral carpet and festive vibes truly captured the spirit of togetherness."

— Cogniza Team & Mentors

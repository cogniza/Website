Structured Curriculum & Learning Modules

A comprehensive, step-by-step learning roadmap designed by veteran practitioners.

Module 1: Product Management Foundations & Mindset
  • Role of a Product Manager vs Project Manager vs Tech Lead
  • The Product Life Cycle (Introduction, Growth, Maturity, Decline)
  • Product-Led Growth (PLG) and identifying customer pain points
Module 2: Product Discovery, User Research & Problem Validation
  • Conducting generative user interviews and empathy mapping
  • Formulating user personas and Customer Journey Maps (CJM)
  • Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD) framework and hypothesis validation
Module 3: Writing Product Requirement Documents (PRDs) & User Stories
  • Structure of an industry-standard PRD (Problem, Goals, Non-goals, User flows, Edge cases)
  • Writing granular user stories with Gherkin acceptance criteria
  • Wireframing user flows in Figma and prototype usability testing
Module 4: Feature Prioritization & Product Roadmapping
  • Prioritization frameworks: RICE (Reach, Impact, Confidence, Effort), Kano model, MoSCoW
  • Building strategic outcome-based product roadmaps (Now-Next-Later)
  • Stakeholder alignment and managing engineering trade-offs
Module 5: Product Analytics, Launch & Capstone SaaS PRD
  • Defining North Star Metric and supporting driver metrics
  • Event tracking with Mixpanel/Amplitude, funnel analysis, and A/B testing
  • Go-To-Market launch plan, release notes, and post-launch retrospectives
  • Capstone: Authoring an end-to-end PRD for an AI-powered collaboration tool

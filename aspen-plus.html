Structured Curriculum & Learning Modules

A comprehensive, step-by-step learning roadmap designed by veteran practitioners.

Module 1: Aspen Plus Architecture & Chemical Thermodynamics
  • Component setup, non-random two-liquid (NRTL), UNIQUAC, and UNIFAC group contribution methods
  • Vapor-Liquid Equilibrium (VLE) and Liquid-Liquid Equilibrium (LLE) validation
  • Electrolyte wizard (eNRTL) for ionic solutions and acid-gas scrubbing
Module 2: Reactor Modeling & Complex Reaction Kinetics
  • Stoichiometric (RStoic), Yield (RYield), Equilibrium (REquil), and Gibbs (RGibbs) reactors
  • Rigorous kinetic reactors: CSTR (RCSTR) and Plug Flow (RPlug) with Arrhenius kinetics
  • Catalyst deactivation and temperature runaway simulations
Module 3: Specialized Separation: Azeotropic & Extractive Distillation
  • Constructing residue curve maps (RCM) and ternary diagrams
  • Extractive distillation of ethanol-water using entrainers (Ethylene glycol)
  • RadFrac column convergence, tray sizing, and structured packing selection
Module 4: Solids Handling & Particle Technology
  • Particle size distributions (PSD), crushers, cyclones, and electrostatic precipitators
  • Crystallizers (CrystS), filters, and rotary drum dryers
  • Handling slurries and phase change operations
Module 5: Economic Evaluation & Capstone Specialty Chemical Synthesis
  • Aspen Process Economic Analyzer (APEA) for capital (CAPEX) and operating (OPEX) costs
  • Carbon footprint and energy integration (Pinch analysis)
  • Capstone: Complete simulation of a 100,000 MT/year Ethyl Acetate synthesis plant

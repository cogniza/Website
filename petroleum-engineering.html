What is the Petroleum Engineering Internship & Training Program?

The Petroleum Engineering program at Cogniza is a specialized technical internship bridging fundamental thermodynamics and fluid mechanics with modern oil, gas, and energy operations. The curriculum covers both upstream exploration and drilling operations as well as downstream chemical process simulation.

With energy security and petrochemicals driving massive industrial growth across India and the Middle East, energy consultants, EPC contractors, and multinational service providers (such as Schlumberger, Baker Hughes, Technip, and L&T Hydrocarbon) require engineering freshers who understand practical field equipment, drilling hydraulics, and simulation flowsheeting.

At Cogniza, students move beyond static textbook equations. You will model real reservoir drive mechanisms, calculate bottom-hole pressures, design casing strings, analyze artificial lift pump performance, and build steady-state chemical process flowsheets in Aspen HYSYS under the guidance of veteran energy engineers.

This internship is designed for engineering undergraduates and recent graduates seeking technical careers across the oil, gas, and petrochemical sectors.

Who should NOT take this petroleum engineering program?

We believe in authentic career orientation so students align their technical strengths with actual industry demands.

  • Students seeking pure web or mobile software development: This is a core engineering program focusing on fluid dynamics, drilling mechanics, and chemical process thermodynamics. If you want to build websites or Android apps, choose our Python Full Stack or Android tracks.
  • Students unwilling to solve engineering physics & chemistry problems: You will analyze phase envelopes, calculate pressure gradients, and design distillation trays. Solid analytical thinking is required.
  • Anyone seeking a passive attendance certificate: Cogniza requires submission of an original reservoir decline analysis or process simulation capstone flowsheet before certifying any intern.

What is the week-by-week curriculum and syllabus?

Real core engineering curriculum covering upstream and downstream operations.

Weeks 1–2: Petroleum Geology & Reservoir Rock/Fluid Physics

Hydrocarbon formation, source rock, migration traps, and seal integrity. Petrophysical rock properties: porosity, permeability, capillary pressure, and wettability. Fluid PVT characterization: bubble point, formation volume factors (Bo, Bg), gas-oil ratio (GOR), and phase envelope behavior.

Weeks 3–4: Drilling Engineering, Rig Mechanics & Well Hydraulics

Rotary drilling rig hoisting, circulating, and rotating systems. Drilling fluids (water-based vs oil-based muds), rheology (Bingham plastic and Power-law models), hydrostatic pressure, swab/surge pressures, casing design fundamentals, and well control kick detection.

Weeks 5–6: Well Completion, Inflow Performance & Artificial Lift

Open-hole vs cased-hole completions, hydraulic fracturing basics, and sand control. Inflow Performance Relationship (IPR) via Vogel's and Darcy's equations. Artificial lift systems: Sucker Rod Pumps (SRP), Electrical Submersible Pumps (ESP), and continuous gas lift selection.

Weeks 7–8: Downstream Refining & Separation Operations

Crude oil characterization, desalting, atmospheric distillation units (ADU/CDU), vacuum distillation (VDU), catalytic cracking (FCC), hydrotreating, and natural gas dehydration/sweetening processes.

Week 9: Process Simulation & Flowsheeting with Aspen HYSYS

Setting up property packages (Peng-Robinson, NRTL), component lists, oil characterization assays, modeling flash drums, heat exchangers, distillation columns, and converging steady-state flowsheet simulations.

Week 10: Energy HSE, Offshore Regulations & Capstone Presentation

Health, Safety & Environmental (HSE) protocols, blowout preventers (BOP), API/ISO safety guidelines, carbon capture and energy transition overview, and final defense of the simulation capstone before senior energy mentors.

What real-world capstone projects will you complete?

Real engineering design calculations and flowsheets to prove technical capability.

Project 1: Reservoir Decline Curve Analysis & In-Place Volumetric Estimation

Brief: Analyze 10 years of historical production data from a mature oil field. Determine original oil in place (OOIP) and forecast remaining economic field life.

1
Material Balance: Formulate Havlena-Odeh straight-line plots to diagnose water drive vs solution gas drive reservoir mechanisms.
2
Decline Curves: Fit Arps decline models (exponential, hyperbolic, harmonic) to derive annual decline rates and estimated ultimate recovery (EUR).
3
Artificial Lift Proposal: Recommend optimal timing for transitioning natural flowing wells to Electrical Submersible Pumps (ESP).

Project 2: Aspen HYSYS Crude Distillation Unit (CDU) Flowsheet Simulation

Brief: Build a steady-state simulation of a 100,000 BPD atmospheric crude distillation column in Aspen HYSYS.

1
Assay Characterization: Enter crude assay TBP distillation curves, define pseudo-components, and select the Peng-Robinson fluid package.
2
Column Convergence: Model furnace preheating, flash zones, side-strippers, and reflux ratios to separate Light Naphtha, Heavy Naphtha, Kerosene, Diesel, and Residue.
3
Optimization: Perform heat exchanger pinch analysis to recover column overhead heat and lower furnace fuel duty by 12%.

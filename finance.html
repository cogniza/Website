What is the Finance & Financial Modeling Internship Program?

The Finance program at Cogniza is an intensive, career-focused internship that transforms theoretical commerce education into professional financial modeling expertise. Students learn how institutional analysts evaluate companies, forecast cash flows, and value businesses using Microsoft Excel and real corporate filings.

In Bangalore's booming corporate finance, venture capital, and private equity landscape, employers require candidates who can immediately open an annual report, extract audited balance sheets and P&L numbers, and construct a dynamic 3-statement forecasting model from scratch. Textbook knowledge alone does not suffice.

Throughout the internship, learners analyze authentic financial disclosures from prominent Indian companies listed on the NSE and BSE. Guided by experienced financial analysts, students construct Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) models, calculate Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC), evaluate comparable market multiples (P/E, EV/EBITDA), and produce professional investment thesis memoranda.

Who is this finance course for and what are the eligibility requirements?

This program is designed for commerce undergraduates, MBA finance candidates, and aspiring financial analysts seeking rigorous spreadsheet modeling and corporate valuation skills. No advanced mathematics or coding background is necessary, though familiarity with basic accounting principles is helpful.

Target Degrees

B.Com, BBA, BBM, MBA (Finance), M.Com, Economics majors, or CA/CFA candidates seeking practical valuation and modeling skills.

Knowledge Prerequisites

Basic understanding of debits, credits, balance sheets, and profit & loss statements. All Excel shortcuts and valuation math are taught from scratch.

Tool Requirements

A standard laptop running Windows or macOS with Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer (Office 365 recommended for modern array formulas).

Who should NOT take this finance program?

Transparent career counseling prevents mismatched expectations. This training is purposefully not designed for every audience.

  • Speculative day traders or crypto enthusiasts: This course does not teach intraday stock charting, penny stock tips, or speculative crypto signals. We focus exclusively on fundamental institutional valuation, corporate FP&A, and financial accounting.
  • Students unwilling to work in spreadsheets: Professional finance lives inside Microsoft Excel. If you dislike structured numerical calculations, formula auditing, or reading annual report footnotes, you will struggle with this workload.
  • Passive certificate seekers: Certificates are only awarded after you submit audited three-statement models and present your valuation findings in a capstone review.

What is the week-by-week curriculum and syllabus?

Real corporate finance syllabus designed by institutional analysts.

Weeks 1–2: Financial Statement Analysis & Accounting Standards

Deconstructing the three core financial statements: Income Statement, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow Statement. Understanding operating, investing, and financing cash flows, working capital dynamics, Ind AS / IFRS nuances, and revenue recognition rules.

Weeks 3–4: Advanced Financial Modeling in Microsoft Excel

Mastering financial modeling best practices in Excel: keyboard shortcuts (zero mouse dependency), dynamic lookup formulas (XLOOKUP, INDEX-MATCH), error trapping (IFERROR), sensitivity tables (Data Tables), Goal Seek, and conditional scenario managers.

Weeks 5–6: Integrated Three-Statement Forecasting Model

Building an integrated dynamic model. Formulating historical revenue drivers, forecasting cost of goods sold (COGS) and SG&A, constructing debt amortization schedules, Capex depreciation schedules, and circularity-free cash sweep mechanisms.

Weeks 7–8: Business Valuation (DCF & Comparable Company Analysis)

Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) framework: Free Cash Flow to Firm (FCFF), Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM), beta calculation, Cost of Debt, and WACC. Terminal Value estimation via Gordon Growth Model and Exit Multiple method. Relative valuation using P/E, EV/EBITDA, and Price/Book multiples.

Week 9: Corporate FP&A & Working Capital Optimization

Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A) workflows: budget vs actual variance analysis, Cash Conversion Cycle (CCC) optimization, inventory turnover, DSO/DPO metric tracking, and Power BI executive summary dashboard design.

Week 10: Equity Research Initiation Report & Capstone Presentation

Writing an institutional-grade equity research initiation report: executive summary, industry tailwinds, business moat, valuation sensitivity analysis, key investment risks, and live defense before senior finance mentors.

What real-world capstone projects will you build?

Tangible, audited financial models you can present directly in job interviews.

Project 1: Dynamic 3-Statement Model for an NSE Listed FMCG Company

Brief: Build a 5-year historical and 5-year forecasted 3-statement financial model for an Indian listed leader (such as Tata Consumer, Dabur, or Marico) using annual report disclosures.

1
Historical Normalization: Clean and standardize 5 years of balance sheets and P&L reports, adjusting for exceptional items.
2
Supporting Schedules: Build dedicated debt repayment schedules, interest expense calculations, and tax schedules.
3
Automated Balancing: Link cash flow changes into cash & cash equivalents so the forecasted balance sheet self-balances without plug numbers.

Project 2: Comprehensive DCF & Relative Valuation Pitch

Brief: Perform an end-to-end intrinsic corporate valuation of an Indian IT or Manufacturing mid-cap firm, defending a BUY/SELL/HOLD recommendation.

1
WACC & FCFF: Derive cost of equity with unlevered industry betas, calculate free cash flow, and perform sensitivity tests across multiple discount rates.
2
Comps Peer Group: Build a comparable company table analyzing EV/Sales, EV/EBITDA, and P/E to benchmark relative target prices.
3
Investment Note: Compile a 15-page equity research pitch book with an executive summary and downside risk mitigation roadmap.
